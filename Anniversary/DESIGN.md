## Brand & Style

This design system channels the dynamism, sonic pulse, and visceral kinetic energy of contemporary Lagosian culture, translated into an avant-garde editorial magazine format. Designed for cultural curators, global music enthusiasts, and creative tastemakers commemorating a monumental 1-year milestone, the experience balances tactile warmth with pristine publication precision.

The visual direction merges **High-Contrast Editorial** with **Tactile Minimalist Modernism**. The atmosphere is deliberate and commanding: razor-sharp visual cadence, expansive negative space balanced against monumental typography, and deliberate rhythmic breaks mirroring the afro-fusion and street-level vibrancy of Lagos. Warm raw grit meets editorial prestige.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in sun-drenched earth, late-night vinyl, and printed editorial stock:

- **Primary (`#D9531E`)**: Terracotta heat. Embodies kinetic life, pulse, and focused interactive states. Used deliberately for focal highlights, dynamic rule lines, badges, and primary calls to action.
- **Secondary (`#E09F3E`)**: Amber glow. Serves as an atmospheric pairing for archival notes, metadata accents, time stamps, and audio play states.
- **Neutral (`#111111`)**: Deep carbon ink. Delivers uncompromising typographic contrast, razor-sharp architectural framing, and authoritative editorial structure.
- **Background Tones**: Base canvas resides on Warm Cream (`#FAFAEC`), emulating heavyweight uncoated archival paper. Elevation layers utilize Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) to let visual features and photography detach effortlessly from the page foundation.

## Typography

The typographical tension pairing is central to this design system: **Newsreader** delivers the literary authority, cadence, and elegance of long-form legacy journals, while **Hanken Grotesk** injects clean, contemporary structural balance with zero distraction.

- Headline hierarchies emphasize italicized literary flourishes for pull quotes, anniversary chapters, and narrative essays.
- Labels, meta categories, issue indicators, and timestamps are always set in `Hanken Grotesk` uppercase with tracked-out letter spacing to evoke curated exhibition placards.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout operates on a flexible 12-column editorial grid on desktop, a 6-column grid on tablet, and a 4-column column flow on mobile:

- **Desktop (1200px+)**: Outer margin of `3.5rem` creates generous magazine white space. Layouts leverage asymmetric column distributions (e.g., 7-column story focus alongside a 5-column archival visual stack) and full-bleed image breaks.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: Margins compress to `2rem` with an 8-to-6 column conversion. Secondary sidebars collapse into sticky inline pull-quotes.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: Canvas margins drop to `1.25rem` with a dynamic 4-column flow. Content shifts strictly vertical, retaining tactile headline impact without horizontal truncation.

## Elevation & Depth

In line with traditional fine-art publishing, this design system avoids simulated blurred drop shadows. Depth and visual hierarchy are instead expressed via **tonal stacking, pure white foreground plates, and fine structural hairline rules**:

- **Ground Level**: Base viewports sit on uncoated Warm Cream (`#FAFAEC`).
- **Elevated Canvas (Articles & Media Modules)**: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) surfaces layered directly on cream create crisp planar elevation.
- **Borders & Dividers**: Crisp, continuous hairline borders (`1px solid rgba(17, 17, 17, 0.12)`) define content boundaries and column separators, evoking physical publication framing.
- **Focus & Floating Components**: Modals, audio players, and floating navigational strips use crisp solid framing combined with an intentional editorial offset shadow: `4px 4px 0px #111111`.

## Shapes

The shape system strictly uses **sharp (0px) geometry**. 

Every perimeter, interactive target, image container, and layout card is unrounded and cut clean. This maintains the physical silhouette of trimmed editorial print stock, broadsheet pages, and cassette/vinyl sleeve inserts.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid Deep Charcoal (`#111111`) fill, Pure White text, sharp 0px corners, uppercase `label-lg` typography. On hover, background shifts instantly to Terracotta (`#D9531E`) with zero easing delay for immediate physical feedback.
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Transparent fill, 1px solid Deep Charcoal border, `#111111` text. On hover, shifts to Warm Cream background with a `#D9531E` border and text.
- **Editorial Text Action**: Raw typographic links featuring an underlined hairline with a 4px gap; transitions to Terracotta on focus.

### Cards & Media Frames
- Built with a Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) background, 1px perimeter border (`rgba(17, 17, 17, 0.15)`), and padded with `space-lg`.
- Images inside cards sit flush to top and side edges or are surrounded by strict cream matting margins. 
- Media captions are rendered in `body-sm` accompanied by an uppercase `label-sm` category tag.

### Chips & Archival Badges
- Compact rectangular tags with 0px radius, 1px hairline border, uppercase `label-md` tracking.
- Active or featured tags leverage Terracotta fill (`#D9531E`) with White text; standard tags use Cream fill with Charcoal text.

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- Unrounded input containers with Pure White ground, bottom-only or full 1px charcoal bounding borders, and `space-md` interior padding.
- Focused state: Border stroke weights to 2px in `#D9531E` without glowing drop-shadow halos.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Sharp rectangular checkboxes (0px radius) and crisp squared radios.
- Inactive: 1.5px solid `#111111` border with a cream center.
- Active: Solid `#D9531E` fill with sharp, high-contrast white glyph indicators.

### Retrospective Timeline & Audio Snippets
- Interactive retrospective markers incorporate a continuous vertical 1px rule anchored by squared node blocks.
- Embedded soundboard/audio components feature raw, functional scrubbing bars rendered in high-contrast charcoal with live terracotta playback progress fills.
